STAGE — SOIL

It starts underground.

Every recommendation begins with what's beneath the crop — moisture, pH, and nutrient levels read continuously so decisions are made on real conditions, not guesswork.

STAGE — CROP

The canopy tells a story.

Growth stage, density and leaf colour are tracked across the season, giving the field a visible, ongoing health baseline.

STAGE — AI DETECTION

Disease, caught early.

Computer-vision models scan leaf imagery for the earliest visible signs of blight, rust and rot — long before it's visible to the eye walking the rows.

STAGE — PEST MONITORING

Cameras that never blink.

Field cameras capture the canopy every few days, flagging pest pressure before it becomes an infestation.

STAGE — NUTRITION

Feed the soil, not the guess.

N-P-K and pH readings are translated into plain, actionable guidance — what's low, what it affects, and what to correct.

STAGE — WEATHER

Knowing what's coming.

Forecasts, rainfall probability and temperature trends are folded into every irrigation and protection decision.

STAGE — IRRIGATION

Water, only when it's needed.

Soil moisture is weighed against the rain forecast before a single drop is recommended — never irrigation for irrigation's sake.

STAGE — AUTOMATION

From insight to action.

Tubewells and irrigation systems can be run manually or switched to AI mode, closing the loop between sensing and action.

STAGE — ALERTS

Nothing slips past.

Disease, pest, weather and hardware events surface as one prioritised alert stream — critical first, noise never.

The platform

Seven systems. One field brain.

Each module works independently, and better together — feeding one shared picture of field health.
